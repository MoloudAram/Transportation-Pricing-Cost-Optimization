# Results

## Overview

This directory contains the outputs generated from the transportation pricing and cost optimization analysis.

## Main Outputs

The analysis can generate:

- Product capacity analysis
- Shipping group summaries
- Transportation cost by province
- Transportation cost by city
- Product-level transportation cost
- Capacity constraint analysis
- Pricing matrices
- Analytical tables and visualizations

## Business Insights

The results are designed to support:

- Transportation pricing standardization
- Cost transparency
- Capacity utilization analysis
- Identification of cost drivers
- Comparison of transportation costs across destinations
- Management decision-making

## Confidentiality

Results containing proprietary company information are not included in the public repository.

Only anonymized or non-confidential outputs may be published.
